# Post-Ship Experiments

These ideas remain deferred until the frozen Tier 1 artifact ships.

1. **Clarification-only ablation:** Add raw context plus targeted questions without the five-part brief to isolate clarification, then compare adaptive dialogue with Tier 1's batched lower bound.
2. **Persistent operator-profile experiment:** Test whether provenance-linked stable preferences reduce clarification burden without reducing decision quality.
3. **Population and personal priors:** Compare generic workflow priors with user-history priors while guarding against stale or paternalistic assumptions.
4. **Mispersonalization safeguards:** Test provenance, confidence, scope, freshness, contradiction handling, and owner override as controls on inferred preferences.
5. **Cross-provider robustness check:** Preserve the Tier 2 plan to repeat Replay 04 across A/B/C for three repeats with another provider's frontier model.
6. **Additional replays and classification:** Add Replays 02, 03, and 05, then defer broader primitive classification and the capability matrix until their evidence exists.
7. **Dubai trajectory:** Add the Dubai corpus as a second real-world decision-and-outcome trajectory after Tier 1.
8. **Design-partner replication:** Apply the method to one bounded workflow from another operator to test whether the observed interface effects transfer.
9. **GUI and desktop execution:** Treat live computer action as a complementary future layer after the static decision and work-product experiment is credible.
