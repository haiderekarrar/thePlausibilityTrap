# Post-Ship Work

These items remain deferred until the exploratory Replay 04 V1 artifact ships. Nothing in this file authorizes a run or makes later execution mandatory.

1. **Replay 01 expansion and posture/claim discipline:** Revisit the operational outcome-execution replay after V1 under the previously approved A/B/C, three-tier, three-repeat architecture or a separately approved redesign. Test whether an agent distinguishes what the operator said, including plans, intentions, negotiation posture, or strategic elicitation, from established world facts and the operator's underlying outcome. The “I am currently renovating” case should test whether sell-as-is or minimal-repair options are wrongly eliminated. Sanitized posture examples may be public; any sensitive personal motivation remains private ground truth.
2. **Clarification-only ablation:** Add unstructured redacted context plus targeted questions without the five-part brief to isolate clarification, then compare adaptive dialogue with the current pilot's batched lower bound.
3. **Persistent operator-profile experiment:** Test whether provenance-linked stable preferences reduce clarification burden without reducing decision quality.
4. **Population and personal priors:** Compare generic workflow priors with user-history priors while guarding against stale or paternalistic assumptions.
5. **Mispersonalization safeguards:** Test provenance, confidence, scope, freshness, contradiction handling, and owner override as controls on inferred preferences.
6. **Cross-provider robustness check:** Preserve the Tier 2 plan to repeat Replay 04 across A/B/C for three repeats with another provider's frontier model; the current pilot does not establish model- or provider-general effects.
7. **Additional replays and classification:** Add Replays 02, 03, and 05, then defer broader primitive classification and the capability matrix until their evidence exists.
8. **Dubai trajectory:** Add the Dubai corpus as a second real-world decision-and-outcome trajectory after V1.
9. **Design-partner replication:** Apply the method to one bounded workflow from another operator to test whether the observed interface effects transfer.
10. **GUI and desktop execution:** Treat live computer action as a complementary future layer after the static decision and work-product experiment is credible.
11. **Test-time search, persistence, reflection, and verification:** Separate six mechanisms that “think longer” claims often combine: a larger per-call search/reasoning budget; persistent continuation that retains the prior trajectory and failures without new owner information; explicit reflection on why the prior strategy failed; fresh independent restarts at approximately matched total compute; continuation after a verifier or human identifies a concrete defect; and demand pressure to continue toward completeness. Precommit rounds, attempts, or total budget and preserve every trajectory rather than continuing until success. Track visible fact recovery, downstream use, source coverage, unsupported claims, claim-versus-fact discipline, retrieval completion, tool-state failures, and decision quality. Citation breadth shows visible coverage, not completeness, correctness, weighting, integration, or reliability. Test demand pressure for both persistence benefits and fabrication, unsupported certainty, premature conclusions, or honesty failures. Transfer from mathematical-search anecdotes remains a hypothesis.
12. **Matched-effort natural request (A′):** Compare a natural request with approximately the same extra search/reasoning opportunity as B, but without requiring B's structured intermediate representation. Current Replay 04 A-versus-B evidence cannot separate benefit from structure itself from benefit from another opportunity to work.
13. **Run-to-run retrieval consistency:** Use enough precommitted independent repetitions to estimate visible recovery variability under an identical treatment, how often evidence is omitted or surfaced but ignored, and how much repetition reduces uncertainty about one run. The current structured outcomes of 1/2, 1/2 with a different communication, and 2/2 are three run-level observations, not a retrieval-rate estimate; do not choose a sample size here.
14. **Larger Replay 04 designs:** Preserve the unexecuted A3/B3/C2/C3 pilot remainder and the 27-attempt Tier 1a grid as deferred historical designs, not unfinished V1 obligations. Before reviving either, explicitly reaffirm or redesign it using the pilot findings rather than mechanically completing a stale plan.
15. **Project-name decision:** Before the first durable public release or outreach, use one small fixed time budget to decide whether “Deal Autopsy” remains the company-independent artifact name. If no clearly better name appears within the cap, keep it and ship. Any later rename should be a repository-wide consistency migration, not a partial rename.
16. **Public user-directed-agent framing:** Explain in plain language whether an AI can recover what the user is trying to accomplish rather than merely optimize the literal requested means, while preserving human authority over consequential decisions. Use objective recovery, intent discovery, evidence organization, ranked unknowns, clarification, decision quality, concrete work products, artifact quality, authority calibration, and verification conditions. The scaffold remains a floor: a model may challenge a false premise, identify a proxy goal, or propose a better non-obvious means. Do not call this a general alignment benchmark or change the experiment to fit the framing.
17. **Persistent-agent implications and counter-risk:** Anchor a company-independent implications section to the observed fact that C1 clarification elicited relevant contemporaneous owner state absent from the supplied context. Treat reduced repeated intent-transfer burden as a hypothesis, alongside the risk that a stale, incorrect, or overconfident user model compounds errors. The present case study does not test persistent memory, personalization, local inference, data sovereignty, or user-owned infrastructure; items 3–5 define the experiments and safeguards needed before making those claims.
